## Scripture source

Choose one edition so every contributor uses the same words. Possible routes include a documented public-domain edition or permission for a modern edition. An older edition means accepting its wording; do not mix editions silently. Verify the actual edition and intended distribution territories.

For a modern Church-sourced text, clarify the proposed use through the [permissions portal](https://permissions.churchofjesuschrist.org/?lang=eng). The [Church website terms](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/learn/legal/terms-of-use/go?lang=eng) distinguish personal use from reposting.

Before adding source text, record edition, publication details, stable source reference, editorial material included/excluded, rights basis, date checked, and any territorial or attribution limits.

## Recordings made with ElevenLabs (optional tool)

The [Music Terms](https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms), checked October 19, 2026 (UTC), restrict Music Libraries in section 4.2. Section 4.8(f) defines this category broadly, including collections offered for download or reuse without payment. Music streaming distribution is treated separately, with plan-dependent permissions.

A free collaborative downloadable archive could fall within that definition. This is an interpretation requiring written clarification for our actual project; it is not a finding that all public listening is prohibited. A paid subscription alone should not be treated as blanket permission for the archive.

Ask ElevenLabs to address volunteer contributions from separate accounts, public listening, streaming, downloads, GitHub Releases or other hosting, attribution, required plans, and any proposed reuse license. Record the permission reference here.

## Contributions

For human recordings, document composition/performance credits and contributor permissions. For AI-assisted recordings, record the actual tool, terms, account plan, and creation date. Obtain permissions for outside performances, voice references, and other materials. Contributors may grant only rights they hold.

Document public listening, downloading, and reuse separately. An accessible link does not authorize copying or relicensing. Do not publish private correspondence or personal details without permission.
