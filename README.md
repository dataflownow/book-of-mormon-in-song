# The Book of Mormon in Song

**The complete Book of Mormon set to beautiful, memorable music—with the exact words, clearly sung.**

This is a volunteer project for people who want to hear, remember, and learn scripture through music. It began with an ElevenLabs experiment setting Mosiah chapter 2 to music. The next step is to bring together people who can create music, listen carefully, and help build a complete collection.

Our musical direction is folk, acoustic, orchestral, inspirational, and sacred. Solo voices are welcome; choral singing is optional. The words lead the music.

## Join in

**You do not need to be a programmer or a musician.**

- **Make music:** [claim a chapter](https://github.com/dataflownow/book-of-mormon-in-song/issues/new?template=01-chapter.md), or join an existing chapter issue.
- **Listen carefully:** compare a recording word for word against the approved source.
- **Help shape the sound:** review pronunciation, clarity, melody, pacing, and accompaniment.
- **Help organize:** improve instructions, maintain chapter records, or help resolve permissions.

Start with [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). You can participate entirely through GitHub issues; pull requests are optional.

## What we are making

- Every scripture word preserved, in its original order.
- Clear, well-enunciated singing suitable for memorization.
- Beautiful, singable melodies that follow the prose.
- Complete chapters, divided into shorter movements where useful.
- Credits, prompts, source information, and review records for every recording.

Different voices and musical approaches are welcome within the [musical guidelines](STYLE_GUIDE.md). Human composition and performance are welcome alongside AI-assisted work.

## First milestone

Complete and independently review five pilot chapters, beginning with Mosiah 2. Use these to refine the contribution process before expanding across the whole book.

See the [chapter tracker](chapters/README.md) and [open issues](https://github.com/dataflownow/book-of-mormon-in-song/issues). A chapter can have alternate versions, with one identified as the main collection version after review.

## Try making a passage

1. Read the [ElevenLabs quick start and copyable prompt](prompts/sacred-folk.md).
2. Use wording you have permission to submit to the generator.
3. Test a short passage before building a whole chapter.
4. Save the exact prompt, lyrics, model, settings, and original audio.
5. [Submit your recording](https://github.com/dataflownow/book-of-mormon-in-song/issues/new?template=02-recording.md) when its sharing permissions are confirmed.

## Current project stage

We are organizing the pilot team. The project text edition and public audio distribution permissions are **not yet finalized**. No recording has been independently approved or added to this repository yet. Existing personal experiments do not establish a project-wide license.

See [Sources and rights](SOURCES_AND_RIGHTS.md) for the decisions being tracked. Please share prompts, ideas, and chapter plans now; publish scripture text and recordings here once the relevant permissions are documented.

This is an independent volunteer project and does not claim Church sponsorship or endorsement.
