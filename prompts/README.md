# Optional music briefs and prompts

Contributors choose any tool or composition/performance approach. This volunteer project is not for profit. Its primary purpose is memorization of the verbatim text, with listening enjoyment a welcome plus.

## A brief for any approach

You can adapt this for an AI music tool, a composer, a singer, or an ensemble:

```text
Set the supplied verbatim Book of Mormon passage to music that honors the sacred intent and sincerity of the text and the spirit of the Book of Mormon.

The primary purpose is to help listeners memorize the exact words. Deliver every word clearly, completely, and in its original order. Do not paraphrase, modernize, omit, substitute, or add lyrics.

Let the musical phrasing accommodate the text. Use musical cues that support learning and recall, and leave enough time for long clauses. Keep the words intelligible throughout. A beautiful and enjoyable listening experience is welcome.

Choose the musical style, voices, instruments, arrangement, and production method that best serve these aims.
```

Use a source whose permissions support your process. For AI-assisted work, put the scripture in the tool's dedicated lyrics field when available, and preserve the exact prompt, input text, tool/model, settings, and original export. For human work, preserve source text, credits, and recording details. Independently check the actual audio against the source.
