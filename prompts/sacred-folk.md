# ElevenLabs sacred folk starter

The suggested prompt for the Mosiah 2 experiment. Record your exact prompt and settings for each submission. This file does not certify an existing recording.

## First test

Select Music v2.5, custom lyrics, and one variation if available. Put music instructions in the description field and the exact scripture in the lyrics field. Mosiah 2:17–19 is a useful short test; try about 90 seconds to two minutes, then adjust for the actual text and delivery.

Preserve wording and punctuation; add line breaks at natural breaths. Avoid automatic lyric rewriting. Use only text you have rights to process. Public posting still depends on the project's [rights decisions](../SOURCES_AND_RIGHTS.md).

## Copyable prompt

```text
An intimate, reverent sacred folk composition with chamber-orchestra accompaniment, setting the supplied English scripture text verbatim for memorization.

One warm, clear solo baritone singing with a natural, unforced tone, precise consonants, fully articulated words, and restrained vibrato. Keep the voice prominent and close, with very little vocal reverberation.

Fingerpicked acoustic guitar, gentle cello, and restrained chamber strings. A timeless acoustic sound, quietly inspirational, tender, dignified, and hopeful. Moderate walking pace, approximately 76 BPM.

Use simple, memorable melodic motifs and a comfortable vocal range. Predominantly syllabic singing: one note per syllable, with natural English word stresses. Let phrase lengths and rhythms follow the sentences. Allow brief breaths at punctuation. Longer clauses may use gently melodic recitative, returning to tuneful phrases at sentence endings.

Sing every supplied word exactly once, in its original order. Preserve all archaic wording. Do not paraphrase, modernize, omit, substitute, or add words. Through-composed structure, without a chorus or repeated refrain.

Keep the accompaniment quiet beneath the words. Begin singing promptly and keep instrumental transitions brief. No pop production, drum kit, electronic instruments, choir, operatic delivery, vocal runs, humming, or ad-libs. Prioritize intelligible text and memorable melody.
```

## Adjustments

| Problem | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Blurred words | Conversational singing, crisp diction, minimal sustained vowels, quieter accompaniment |
| Reading over music | A distinct singable melody in every phrase, with recurring melodic shapes |
| Rushed or missing words | More time or smaller movements, preserving all words |
| Too much decoration | One note per syllable; restrained vibrato; no vocal runs |
| Folk sound does not fit | Sacred art song for solo voice, piano, and cello |

Voice and instrumentation can change within the [musical guidelines](../STYLE_GUIDE.md). Check every word in the actual audio before memorizing it.

Controls and terms may change; see [ElevenLabs music documentation](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/products/music). Generation is probabilistic: a saved prompt does not guarantee identical future audio.
