# Contributing

You can make music, verify words, review musical clarity, or help organize. You can participate entirely through GitHub issues; pull requests are optional.

## Claim a chapter

Search existing issues for the chapter. Comment to volunteer, or open a **Claim a chapter** issue if none exists. A maintainer confirms the lead. Start with one chapter and give an update after about two weeks; if your plans change, we can reopen the assignment.

Long chapters can become several movements. Agree on verse boundaries in the chapter issue so the combined recording has no gaps or unintended overlap. Alternate interpretations are welcome.

## Use the agreed source

Read [SOURCES_AND_RIGHTS.md](SOURCES_AND_RIGHTS.md). The project edition is still pending. Organizing and sharing prompts can proceed now; use only text you have rights to process for experiments, and document permissions before posting scripture text or audio publicly.

Once selected, use the project source. Change line breaks for phrasing, not words, spelling, or order. Do not sing verse numbers or editorial headings unless explicitly included in the chapter plan.

## Make a short test

Follow the [musical guidelines](STYLE_GUIDE.md) and try the [starter prompt](prompts/sacred-folk.md). Establish a sound with a short passage before spending credits on a whole chapter.

Save the exact prompt, lyrics input, model/version, generation date, settings, and original export. No subscription is required to participate: listening and reviewing are valuable. Contributors choose their own costs; reimbursement is not promised.

## Submit

Use the **Submit a recording** issue template. Include the chapter issue, verse range, source edition, permitted listening link, credits, full prompt, production details, and text/audio rights status. If permissions are unresolved, submit metadata and the prompt without audio or scripture text.

Use [record-template.md](chapters/record-template.md) for the permanent record. Keep large audio masters out of ordinary Git history; maintainers will select a distribution route after permissions are documented. Never publish credentials, API keys, private correspondence, or personal contact information.

## Review

Use [REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md](REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md). Someone other than the creator verifies every word against the approved source. A second reviewer checks pronunciation, intelligibility, melody, and production; two independent reviewers are preferred.

Record exact versions and timestamped findings. Review the audio itself, not just displayed lyrics. Automated transcription may flag problems but does not replace listening.

Maintainers approve a recording only after text review, musical review, complete chapter coverage, and publication permissions are confirmed.

## Track progress

The chapter issue is the working record. Maintainers update [the tracker](chapters/README.md) at milestones:

Available → Claimed → Draft submitted → Text review → Musical review → Approved.

Track rights separately as pending or cleared. Audio changes require renewed checks of affected passages and transitions.

Be welcoming and specific. People with different religious backgrounds can participate through shared care for the text and music. Keep chapter threads focused on making and reviewing the collection.
